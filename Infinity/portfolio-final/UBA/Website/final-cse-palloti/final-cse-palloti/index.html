Project Title : Design and development of automated urea spraying drone for gps mapped area in Khatmari village.

Project Objective : Making Students aware of the information about the drone Technology.

Project Status : In-progress

Project Description :

The environment in the Vidarbha region is unpredictable for farming. Certainty of rainfall and appropriate environmental conditions are essential for optimal plant growth and improved crop yields. In conventional cultivation systems, farmers must closely monitor rainfall and light intensity, which vary for different crops.
The dearth of water and uncertainty in rainfall in the Vidarbha region are the primary reasons for low or no crop yields. This situation has persisted year after year, leading farmers to take loans they cannot repay on time, resulting in depression and, tragically, suicides. If technology could assist in creating the appropriate environmental conditions for crops, farmers could improve their yields and become economically stable. Thus, the proposed project aims to provide a comprehensive solution for development and time-saving for villagers, reducing urgency as well.
To implement this drone, our requirements include a DJI Pro drone with a pre-installed mini camera, capable of 3.2 to 20 MP, with prices ranging from 20,000 to 30,000 INR. A simple tank with a capacity of 4 to 5 liters will be connected to the drone, with a maximum cost of 6,000 to 8,000 INR, depending on the manufacturer. Additional components, such as sprinklers, pipes, and fillers, are estimated to cost around 3,000 to 4,000 INR.
An operator using this drone can easily cover up to 3 acres of land in just 15 to 20 minutes. The drone's battery can be recharged in 30 to 40 minutes. The main advantages include reduced wastage of fertilizers and water, time savings, and decreased exposure to harmful pesticides (low-risk exposure). Additionally, the drone is equipped with obstacle radar to minimize the chances of accidents and damage.
