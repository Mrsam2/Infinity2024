Decode Derby
Welcome to Decode Derby!
The Legacy Event of Infinity
Dive into an engaging event where teams compete in drawing, guessing, and coding challenges. Test your creativity as you draw and guess keywords, navigate hint-based problem-solving, and tackle coding tasks. Work together to showcase your skills in a fun, competitive environment.

Round 1: 🎨 Scribble Showdown ✏️ In the first round, teams will engage in a lively drawing challenge. Each team selects a representative who will choose a word to illustrate. The representative will have a limited time ⏳ to draw the chosen word while their teammates try to guess it. With five chances to get it right, the pressure is on! 💥 If the team can’t guess correctly, the opposing team has the opportunity to swoop in and steal the point. 🦅 It’s a race against time and creativity! 🏃‍♂️💨✨
Round 2: 🕵️‍♂️ Codenames Clash 🔍 The action continues with a strategic twist in the second round. Teams will face off in a grid of 25 words, with some secretly assigned to the Red Team 🔴 and others to the Blue Team 🔵. Each team appoints a Spymaster 🕵️‍♀️ who holds the key to which words belong to whom. Taking turns, the Spymasters will give clever clues 🗝️ to their Operatives, guiding them to guess their team’s words while avoiding the opponent’s. 🚫 The first team to correctly identify all their words wins this round and secures bragging rights! 🏆🎉🥳
Round 3: 🕵️‍♀️ Mystery Round 🔮 In the grand finale of Decode Derby, teams will enter the Mystery Round. Here, participants must rely on their deduction skills and creative thinking 🧠💡 to solve a series of puzzles or riddles based on the previous rounds. 🧩 Each correct answer earns points, and the team with the highest score at the end of the event is crowned the Decode Derby champions! 👑🏅✨🎊
